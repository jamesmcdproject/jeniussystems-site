how the work goes

  1. start on site.

    the spec comes from watching the work get done. the people with the problem are the product team.

  2. build inside their walls.

    their hardware, their constraints, their data staying where it is. the environment is a requirement, not an obstacle.

  3. let the fleet build.

    agents design, implement, test and review in gated waves. one human owns the direction and the outcome.

  4. say what's true.

    live means live. pilot means pilot. nothing here is for sale and there is nothing to sign up for.

the proving ground

games are where the fleet gets tested on the hard part: real time, real people, a living room that will tell you immediately when it isn't fun.

  • couch critters ↗ LIVE

    a co-op party platformer inspired by TeamO's PICO PARK. the TV runs the game; every phone becomes a gamepad — scan a QR code and you're in.

  • kart rush ↗ LIVE

    a mario-kart-style racer for the living room. the TV runs the race; scan the QR code and your phone is the wheel — tilt to steer, flick to drift, up to four drivers split-screen, AI fills the grid. friends far away can join from their own TV.
