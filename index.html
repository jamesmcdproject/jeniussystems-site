how the work goes

  1. start with my own work.

    the list comes from my own desk: the draw, the close, the tax exposure. I'm the first user of everything here.

  2. the numbers have to tie.

    this is accounting. penny-exact or it isn't done, and every number traces back to its source.

  3. let the fleet build.

    agents design, implement, test and review in gated waves. one human owns the direction and the outcome.

  4. say what's true.

    live means live. pilot means pilot. in build means not done yet. nothing for sale, nobody for hire.
