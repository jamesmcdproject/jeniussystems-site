03 · PLATFORM · CASE NOTES

BMC Development

A construction-finance platform for a real-estate investment firm's construction team. Invoices arrive as PDFs; what leaves is an approved, penny-exact, export-ready draw package.

What it does

  • Invoice intake: PDF upload with OCR — vendor, amounts, and line items extracted, then coded to projects and cost codes.
  • Control: per-project approval policies (two-step or threshold-based, distinct approvers) with a vendor compliance gate before money moves on paper.
  • Budgets: dual budget views per project, penny-exact splits across cost-code sets — budget-vs-actual at a glance.
  • Draws: draft-to-final draw assembly with auto-joined approved invoices, safely reversible before finalization.
  • Exports: job-cost reports, invoice bundles numbered to match, and tracker workbooks — the package a lender expects.

How it's built

  • Self-hosted: runs in Docker on hardware the firm controls; financial data never leaves it.
  • Test-driven: an 800-plus automated test suite, with money-handling paths written and reviewed at the strictest tier of the studio's process.
  • Agent-built: designed and implemented by the jenius Systems agent fleet under human direction, in review-gated waves.
Why there's no demo link: this is a private pilot handling real construction finances. It isn't publicly accessible, by design. The public record is this page.
